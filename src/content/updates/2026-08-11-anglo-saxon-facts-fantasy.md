Ten Anglo-Saxon facts that sound like fantasy — but are completely true.

Among them: **wyrd**, often translated as fate. Anglo-Saxon poetry insists that although fate is inevitable, honour lies in how one faces it. *Wyrd bið ful āræd* — fate remains wholly inexorable.
