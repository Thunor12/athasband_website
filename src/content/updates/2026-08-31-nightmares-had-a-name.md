There was a time when nightmares had a name.

In the Old English imagination, the darkness of sleep could take the shape of a creature — something that came in the night, pressed against the body, and left you unable to move or breathe.

The word survived. The creature became folklore. And somehow, we still call our darkest dreams by its name.

Would you have believed there was something waiting for you in the dark?
