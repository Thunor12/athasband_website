Do you know the battle of Maldon?

In 991, on the banks of the Blackwater in Essex, the Anglo-Saxon ealdorman Byrhtnoth faced a Viking army. The English were defeated. Byrhtnoth was killed. And yet the Old English poem *The Battle of Maldon* still remembers loyalty, honour, and the bond between lord and warrior.

The Chronicle confirms the death; the poem gives the drama. Even the manuscript barely survived the Cotton Library fire — and what remains still feels strangely alive.
