We talk about the Elder Futhark all the time — and far less about the runes used in Anglo-Saxon England.

The Futhorc grew from that older system and was reshaped for the sounds of Old English. New runes were added; the tradition expanded with language, place, and people.

Runes were never one fixed alphabet. Anglo-Saxon inscriptions deserve that attention — archaeology, language, and early medieval history carved in the same strokes.
