There is something haunting about Friedrich’s landscapes.

Solitary figures, endless mountains, mist swallowing the horizon — the feeling of standing before something vast and unknowable. That vision of the sublime still lives in dark fantasy, gothic art, and worlds built around ancient ruins.

For Aþas, we draw from the same feeling: nature immense, beautiful, and threatening; stones that remember what men have forgotten; mist that might hide something waiting beyond the horizon.
